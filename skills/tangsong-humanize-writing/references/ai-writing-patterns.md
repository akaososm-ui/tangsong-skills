# AI Writing Patterns

## Core Standard

Less perfect, more specific.

The goal is not to make the text casual. The goal is to remove the feeling that every sentence was optimized for general correctness. Keep the facts, lower the posture, and let the writing sound like it came from a person who has actually handled the problem.

## Diagnosis Before Rewrite

Before editing, scan for three layers:

1. Content inflation: the text makes ordinary things sound historically important, strategic, disruptive, or symbolic without evidence.
2. Structure inflation: every paragraph is evenly arranged, often as three points, with symmetrical headings and neat closing slogans.
3. Voice flattening: the text has no first-person judgment, no concrete situation, no friction, and no natural change of rhythm.

If the original is already clear, do targeted edits. Do not rewrite for the sake of rewriting.

## Common Signals And Moves

| Signal | Why It Feels Like AI | Better Move |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "标志着", "凸显", "象征", "奠定基础", "关键节点" | The sentence forces macro meaning onto a small fact. | Say what actually changed, who uses it, or what it solves. |
| "多家媒体报道", "专家认为", "行业报告显示" without source | It borrows authority without giving evidence. | Name the exact source, or remove the claim. |
| "不只是 X, 更是 Y" repeated | The reversal creates easy drama. | Keep only the direct judgment. |
| Three parallel adjectives or three benefits | It feels generated to be complete. | Keep the strongest point, merge the rest, or make the list uneven. |
| "此外", "值得注意的是", "综上", "从...到..." | Connectors are doing the thinking for the paragraph. | Delete or replace with plain transitions. |
| "赋能", "闭环", "提效", "升级", "沉淀", "体系化" | Useful business words become empty when not tied to action. | Add the exact behavior: save, tag, compare, retrieve, rewrite, publish. |
| Every paragraph ends with a summary line | The draft sounds like a lesson handout. | Stop earlier, or end with a concrete next action. |
| Vertical bold-title list | It looks like generated presentation notes. | Convert to prose, or keep only headings readers truly need. |
| Excessive bold, emoji, long dashes | Decoration replaces voice. | Remove unless it carries publishing function. |
| Over-apology or AI chat residue | The assistant voice leaked into the article. | Delete phrases such as "希望对你有帮助", "以下是", "如果你需要". |
| Vague optimistic ending | It avoids a real conclusion. | End with a small action, a boundary, or a concrete consequence. |

## Tang Song Rewrite Moves

Use these when the draft is for社群日课、公众号、朋友圈、课程说明、咨询交付:

- Open from a real action: "这两天我在整理自己的 Obsidian..." is stronger than "知识库对内容创作者至关重要".
- Use reader-facing scenes: "临到要写稿, 翻不到之前那句话" is stronger than "信息检索效率较低".
- Keep the business purpose close: show how the note later becomes a选题、脚本、方案、交付材料, or客户案例.
- Replace perfect frameworks with a living process: collect -> classify -> retrieve -> reuse -> update.
- Let examples carry the point. Do not explain every concept twice.
- Keep a few plain sentences. Not every paragraph needs a golden sentence.

## Boundaries

Do not invent:

- client names, dates, revenue numbers, course results, screenshots, or private cases;
- personal emotion the user did not imply;
- citations or "real feedback" that were not provided.

When important information is missing, use one of these:

- mark it as `待补`;
- keep the sentence general;
- ask for the real detail if the rewrite depends on it.
